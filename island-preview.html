Dynamic Island: FRH Live Activities

An app can't send ordinary notifications into the Dynamic Island. What it can put there is a Live Activity: a small, live view of something happening right now, which FRH updates from its server. Below is everything FRH would show, in each form iOS gives an activity. Try it on the phone in Final v19: the panel's "Dynamic Island" group, or close the app during the live visit.

Where it shows

In the island only while the patient is outside FRH (home screen, another app). Always on the Lock Screen. Opening FRH hides it there, since the app itself shows the same thing.

Four forms

Compact around the camera; minimal when two activities run; expanded on a long press; alert, a short expanded pop with sound when an update matters.

How it updates

FRH's server sends a Live Activity push (Apple ActivityKit) at each step. The phone never polls. An activity runs up to 8 hours, then stays on the Lock Screen for a while after it ends.

Not for the island

Chat messages, reminders, offers and receipts stay ordinary notifications (banners). Phones without a Dynamic Island show only the Lock Screen card.

1 · Home visit Main one

Starts when the team is assigned and ends after the visit. Same people and times as the prototype: Dr. Layla and Grace, arriving about 5:24 PM, gate code from the booking note.

2 · IV drip

A countdown while the nurse runs the drip, so a resting patient sees the time left without unlocking the app.

3 · Video consultation

Waiting in the queue, then an alert with Join the call the moment the doctor is ready.

4 · Lab results

From sample collection to results. The alert comes once the doctor has reviewed them, never before. The last tile shows two activities at once: the visit attaches to the island and the lab result takes the bubble.

All names, times and figures are placeholders, as in the rest of the prototype. Colours: the island is always black, FRH red is lifted to #FF5A5F so it reads on black, and iOS green marks here, ready and done.
